    FAQs

    What families ask first.

    How much does home care cost?

    It depends on the type of care and the number of hours. We give you a clear written quote after a short conversation, with no obligation.

    Is there a minimum visit length?

    Visits are a minimum of 4 hours. Many families begin with a few visits a week and adjust from there.

    How quickly can care start?

    Often within [TIMEFRAME] of your first call, depending on the care needed and caregiver availability.

    Will we have the same caregiver each time?

    We aim for consistency. We match a primary caregiver to your loved one and introduce any backup caregiver ahead of time.

    How are your caregivers screened?

    Every caregiver completes a vulnerable sector check, reference checks and credential verification. Our nurses are registered with the College of Nurses of Ontario.

    Can care change as needs change?

    Yes. We review the plan with you regularly and can add hours, services or nursing support whenever it's needed.

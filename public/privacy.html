Dela J Care Solutions ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how we protect it. We handle personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, where it applies, Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA).

What we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect the information you choose to give us: your name, phone number, email address (optional), the topic of your request, and any message you write. We ask that you do not include detailed medical information in the form; we will discuss care needs with you by phone.

When you apply for a job through our careers page, we also collect the position you're applying for, your experience, availability, work areas, transportation, and any resume you choose to upload.

Our website host (Cloudflare) may process basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the site securely and to protect it from spam and abuse.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and arrange a call back.
  • To discuss and provide home care or staffing services you ask about.
  • To consider your application if you are applying to work with us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

Form submissions are delivered to our business email by our website provider. We share personal information only with people at Dela J Care Solutions who need it to respond to you, with service providers who help us run our business under confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.
